\section{System architecture}\label{sec:architecture}

\Cref{fig:architecture} shows the deployed pipeline. Three independent lanes produce the three row blocks of the
submission; Task 3 has no lane of its own and is scored by the evaluator from the state rows. The lanes share
nothing at inference time: in particular the offline state reconstruction never feeds a queue forecast, which keeps
the Task 2 information set to its twelve legal slots. Every learned artefact (trees, profiles, factor and covariance
models, the queue classifier, the onset event pool and the trajectory library) is fitted on the training split.

\begin{figure}[t!]
\centering
\input{figures/architecture_diagram.tex}
\caption{Deployed architecture of ES4. TF3 is identical except that it lacks the gap-shell recentering stage and uses
the earlier Q46 classifier in place of EQ1. Colours mark tasks. Solid arrows are data flow at inference; the dashed
arrow is the evaluator's own computation. Nothing flows between lanes.}\label{fig:architecture}
\end{figure}
